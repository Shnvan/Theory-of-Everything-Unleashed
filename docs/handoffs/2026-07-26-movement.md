### The measurement that changed the design

D-028 anticipated an "auto-sprint threshold" constant. **It is not needed, and this was measured rather than assumed:** in a play session, a move vector of magnitude 0.5 produced 8.00 speed against magnitude 1.0's 16.17 — a ratio of 0.495. The engine already scales speed by input magnitude, so a part-deflected stick walks by itself and full deflection reaches the cap. "Automatic at full movement input" needs no constant to express.

Two values instead of three, and no threshold that could drift. D-028 has been corrected in place rather than left to mislead.

**Side finding worth keeping:** `Humanoid.MoveDirection` is normalised and reported magnitude `1.000` even for a half-deflected input. It cannot be used to read stick deflection. Nothing here needed that, but `InputController.GetMovementDirection` has a branch that assumes otherwise — harmless today, wrong if something later relies on it.

### Verification

- Magnitude-scaling measurement: **RUN**, and it gated the design.
- Unit tests: **PASS, 42/42**.
- Static gates: **PASS**, all four.
- **Live play, driven over MCP:** `WalkSpeed` 16 at rest → **8** with `Shift` held → **16** on release. Input to behaviour, proven rather than asserted.
- **Respawn mid-hold: PASS.** Held `Shift`, killed the character, and the new `Humanoid` came back at 8 — it did not latch to sprint. This was the failure I expected most, and it is the same class as the S10 bug.
- Device emulation / touch: **NOT RUN.** Auto-sprint on a real thumbstick, and specifically part-deflection walking, has not been felt by a person.
- Two clients: **NOT RUN.**

### Risks and blockers

- **Speed is client-side and not exploit-resistant.** A client sets its own `WalkSpeed`. Normal for a Roblox prototype, and `TECHNICAL_ARCHITECTURE.md` gives the server authority over combat state rather than speed — but it is not defended, and nothing here pretends otherwise.
- **16 will probably feel slow.** It is the engine default, kept so the arena's crossing-time measurement stays valid. Tuning it up is expected — but it invalidates P-008 and the `6.15s per 100 studs` figure in the arena doc, so re-measure when you do.
- **`Shift`-to-walk is now feelable and still unjudged.** D-028 inverted a convention players know. This is the first build where you can tell whether that was a mistake, and it is cheap to reverse now.
- Dash remains unimplemented, so M1's exit condition ("a player can move, dash, ...") is not met.
- Everything outstanding from prior sessions stands: device acceptance walk, two-client test, `REF-ARENA-001` re-signing, the Q-024 attribution line.
