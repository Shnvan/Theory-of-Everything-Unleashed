# IP, Identity, and Content Safety

This is a practical release-risk checklist, not legal advice or legal clearance.

## Core rule

Using a title instead of a real person's name is branding, not a legal shortcut. A character may still identify a person through the combined face, biography, voice, clothing, quotations, equipment, events, and marketing.

## Historical fighters

The initial historical roster uses figures who died long ago:

- Isaac Newton.
- Archimedes.
- Leonardo da Vinci.
- Galileo Galilei.
- Ada Lovelace.
- Charles Darwin.
- Ibn al-Haytham.

Historical facts and scientific ideas are different from the creative sources that depict them. A fact may be usable while a modern photograph reproduction, illustration, film costume, recording, voice performance, logo, music track, or game design remains protected.

For each historical fighter:

- Create an original stylized model and silhouette.
- Use references whose copyright and source are documented.
- Do not trace or closely reproduce a protected image or another game's design.
- Write original dialogue.
- Avoid implying endorsement by a family, estate, university, museum, foundation, or scientific organization.
- Review name, likeness, publicity, privacy, trademark, copyright, and local-law issues before public marketing.

## Original science archetypes

These five characters are fully original:

- Storm Architect.
- Radiant Pioneer.
- Spacetime Savant.
- Atomic Catalyst.
- Cosmic Theorist.

They may use broad scientific concepts. They must not be marketed or designed as disguised versions of Tesla, Curie, Einstein, Oppenheimer, Hawking, a living actor, or a recognizable film portrayal.

Change more than a name:

- Face and body.
- Hair and clothing.
- Nationality and biography.
- Era and occupation.
- Voice and speech pattern.
- Quotations and signature phrases.
- Signature equipment and mobility aids.
- Marketing keywords, thumbnails, and tags.

Internal test:

> If five unprompted testers immediately name the same modern person, treat the character as identifiable and redesign or seek qualified clearance.

## Project and fighter titles

Before public release:

- Search Roblox for confusingly similar experience names.
- Search relevant trademark databases and web use for `Theory of Everything: Unleashed` and paid product/fighter titles.
- Do not assume a phrase is safe merely because it is common or not copyrightable.
- Record the search date, countries checked, result, and decision.
- Obtain qualified legal advice if a material conflict appears.

The development title remains usable internally while this review is pending.

## Asset provenance ledger

Every external or generated asset needs a record.

Store operational records in the [Asset Provenance Ledger](ASSET_PROVENANCE_LEDGER.md). Do not approve an asset from this field template alone.

| Field | Record |
|---|---|
| Asset name and internal ID | Exact identifier |
| Type | Model, mesh, texture, image, animation, audio, font, plugin, code |
| Creator/source | Person, marketplace listing, generator, or original |
| Source URL or file | Recoverable reference |
| License/permission | Exact terms or written permission |
| Commercial use allowed | Yes/no/unclear |
| Modification allowed | Yes/no/unclear |
| Attribution required | Exact text |
| Purchase receipt | Location |
| Date obtained | Date |
| Reviewer and status | Approved/hold/replace |

Do not rely on a Creator Store listing title as proof that the uploader owned the asset.

Every non-zero asset, plugin, extension, subscription, commission, or tool cost also requires separate advance user approval. State the exact price and currency, recurring terms, rights, purpose, risks, and best free alternative before spending.

## Combat and Final Proof

The intended presentation is stylized science fantasy:

- No blood.
- No dismemberment.
- No organs or realistic body damage.
- No torture or lingering suffering.
- No realistic execution.
- No recreation of real atrocities.
- No copied “Finish Him” wording, sound design, camera sequence, animation, or fatality.
- Final Proof uses a short energy, portal, transformation, launch, or scientific effect.

Review the finished experience and all uploaded assets against Roblox's current content-maturity questionnaire. Marketing thumbnails must follow the same safety boundary.

## Release checklist

- [ ] Development title and fighter/product titles searched.
- [ ] Character clearance record completed.
- [ ] Every visual reference and quotation has a source.
- [ ] Every model, texture, image, animation, audio track, font, plugin, and code dependency has provenance.
- [ ] Original modern archetypes pass the unprompted-identification test.
- [ ] No asset copies a film, actor, franchise design, or competitor presentation.
- [ ] Content-maturity questionnaire reviewed against the final build.
- [ ] Final Proof effects reviewed frame by frame.
- [ ] Experience description avoids false endorsement.
- [ ] Store items clearly describe contents.
- [ ] Holds are removed or the affected content is excluded.
