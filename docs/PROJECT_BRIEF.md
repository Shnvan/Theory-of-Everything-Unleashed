# Project Brief

**Name:** Theory of Everything: Unleashed  
**Genre:** public free-for-all battleground  
**Platform:** Roblox  
**Status:** pre-production / combat prototype  
**Business goal:** build a commercially viable Roblox experience, beginning with evidence that the combat loop retains players

## Player promise

> Enter a chaotic science arena, control a legendary mind or original science archetype, weaponize a field of discovery, and create spectacular physics-driven fights with other players.

The player fantasy is:

> “I am a genius whose theories have become superpowers.”

## Audience

- Broad Roblox action-game players.
- Players who recognize or are curious about famous historical thinkers.
- Players who enjoy readable combos, ragdoll reactions, dramatic abilities, and funny public-server moments.
- Keyboard/mouse and mobile players.

Historical knowledge is not required. Recognition is the hook, not a quiz.

## Founder constraints

| Constraint | Current fact |
|---|---|
| Team | Solo developer |
| Luau level | Advanced |
| Time | About 60 hours per week available |
| Initial cash budget | About US$100 |
| Existing assets | No finished models, animations, art, audience, or tester pool |
| Primary goal | Revenue |

These constraints favor a reusable combat foundation, one arena, one polished fighter first, and placeholder presentation until the loop proves fun.

## Product pillars

### 1. Science becomes spectacle

Every fighter should be understandable through a scientific field: gravity pulls, light refracts, machines unfold, electricity chains, and space bends.

### 2. Responsive combat with dramatic payoff

Basic actions must respond quickly. Larger abilities may be cinematic, but must not remove control from the whole server or make public-FFA combat unreadable.

### 3. Social chaos

Fights should create stories: interruptions, knockback, ragdolls, narrow escapes, short KO effects, and eventually limited reactive scenery or props.

### 4. Fair competition

Skill, timing, positioning, matchup knowledge, and decision-making determine combat. Monetization must not sell combat power.

### 5. Small systems, reusable content

Universal movement, combat states, hit validation, meter, KO credit, and mobile input are built once. Fighters supply data and character-specific behavior without duplicating the foundation.

## Core loop

1. Join a public arena.
2. Select a fighter.
3. Enter combat immediately.
4. Deal damage, earn KOs and assists, and fill Discovery Meter.
5. Activate a temporary Breakthrough.
6. Die, respawn quickly, and re-engage.
7. Later: earn mastery and cosmetic rewards.

There are no formal rounds in the primary mode.

## Identity

- **Tone:** stylized science-superhero action with room for physics comedy.
- **Violence:** non-gory, non-realistic, and appropriate for a broad Roblox audience.
- **Naming:** fighters appear under original titles such as `Gravity Sovereign`, even when based on a safely usable historical figure.
- **Signature terms:**
  - **Discovery Meter:** ultimate meter.
  - **Breakthrough:** temporary awakened state or empowered moveset.
  - **Final Proof:** short character-themed KO effect.

## Experience shape

- One contained circular public arena with room for eight-player combat and distributed landmarks.
- Target maximum of eight players.
- Continuous combat and quick respawn.
- Practice dummy support for early and low-population testing.
- A session leaderboard for KOs and assists.
- No forced queue before normal play.

## Non-goals for the prototype

- Recreating Jujutsu Shenanigans, Mortal Kombat, Tekken, or another game's exact moves, presentation, maps, UI, audio, terminology, or character designs.
- A strict 1v1 ranked fighter.
- A large destructible city.
- A full 12-character roster.
- Story mode, trading, clans, gacha, or a battle pass.
- Realistic biographies or simulations of real historical tragedies.

## Product test

The concept is worth expanding only if the graybox version creates the response:

> “Let me fight again,”

not merely:

> “This could look cool after it has art.”
