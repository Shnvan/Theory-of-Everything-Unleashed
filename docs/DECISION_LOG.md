| D-031 | 2026-07-26 | LOCKED | The combat-state transition table, implemented in `src/shared/Combat/CombatStateMachine.luau`. Six ordered rules: **(1)** a state never replaces itself; **(2)** any state may enter `Dead`; **(3)** `Dead` is otherwise terminal, with `SpawnProtected` the only exit; **(4)** `Blocking` may be entered **only from `Neutral` or `SpawnProtected`**; **(5)** `Neutral` is reachable from every living state, as the universal recovery target; **(6)** otherwise `CombatTypes.isStrongerThan` decides. `Dash` is additionally permitted while `Ragdolled`, as the escape. `Walk` drives no state and is permitted whenever alive. | `docs/COMBAT_SYSTEM.md` fixed the ten states and their priority order but never defined which transitions are legal — it only required that they be defined *centrally*. ## How to add a decision

1. Describe the problem and alternatives.
2. State whether the change affects a LOCKED direction or a DEFAULT.
3. Record the user's choice, date, and rationale.
4. Update affected documents and `TASKS.md`.
5. Mark the old choice SUPERSEDED instead of deleting its history.
