# Omniscience Coliseum Exhibit Accuracy

**Status:** LOCKED deferred production specification; research and modeling not started

**Applies after:** the M4 prototype gate passes and the project explicitly continues into production

**Decision:** D-017

**Source review date:** 2026-07-23

## Purpose

This document defines the future museum-accuracy pass for the 24 static exhibits in **The Omniscience Coliseum**. It does not authorize production art during M1 and does not invalidate the accepted D-016 Studio-native prototype.

The production pass must replace invented visual mechanisms with one of:

- a **named replica** based on a specific catalogued artifact;
- a **scientific reconstruction** based on authoritative data, measurements, or engineering principles; or
- a **historical portrayal** based on traceable, rights-safe visual evidence.

Do not fill gaps with decorative machinery and present it as real. If an essential measurement, mechanism, or license cannot be verified, put that exhibit on hold.

## Compatibility and scale

- Inspect and preserve every existing Studio Model name; this document changes visible presentation, not internal identity.
- Preserve the existing exhibit footprint, simplified collision shell, spawn clearance, containment, and required `Z=70` traversal lane.
- Preserve real internal proportions and construction, then uniformly enlarge the complete display to suit the monumental arena.
- Record both real dimensions and Roblox display dimensions. A display-scale change is not permission to distort individual components.
- Keep all production visuals anchored, static, non-colliding, non-touching, and non-queryable. Existing broad collision shells remain the physical representation.
- Original fighter-title monuments remain separate from the historically grounded scientist-statue exhibit.

### Conflict: the Curie statue and Radiant Pioneer

A named Marie Curie monument standing in the arena where **Radiant Pioneer** — the radiant-fields-and-decay original character — fights invites exactly the association [CHARACTER_ROSTER.md](CHARACTER_ROSTER.md) and [IP_CONTENT_SAFETY.md](IP_CONTENT_SAFETY.md) forbid, and it fails this project's own test: "If five unprompted testers immediately name the same modern person, treat the character as identifiable and redesign."

Do not model the Hall of Minds statues until **Q-021** is resolved. Options are to substitute a different figure, drop the statue, or retheme Radiant Pioneer. Raised as S4 in [PROJECT_AUDIT_2026-07-25.md](PROJECT_AUDIT_2026-07-25.md).

## Factual museum card

Each exhibit receives exactly one smaller, center-facing factual card separate from its large real-name stone. It must remain readable without `AlwaysOnTop` and include:

1. exact artifact, experiment, dataset, or display name;
2. maker, discoverer, institution, or data authority;
3. relevant creation, experiment, or data-release date;
4. one-sentence purpose using original wording;
5. documented real dimensions or scientific scale;
6. Roblox display dimensions and uniform scale factor;
7. `REPLICA`, `RECONSTRUCTION`, `DATA VISUALIZATION`, or `HISTORICAL PORTRAYAL`;
8. short source credit and a stable source identifier; and
9. a qualification such as `SCHEMATIC`, `NOT TO SCALE`, or `SELECTED DATA` when required.

Full URLs and license records belong in the research dossier and [Asset Provenance Ledger](ASSET_PROVENANCE_LEDGER.md), not in tiny in-world text.

## Research dossier gate

Before any exhibit is modeled, its dossier must record:

| Field | Required evidence |
|---|---|
| Internal Studio identity | Exact existing Model path and name |
| Visible name | Approved real-name stone text |
| Classification | Replica, reconstruction, data visualization, or historical portrayal |
| Primary anchor | Institution, object/dataset identifier, stable URL, and access date |
| Corroboration | A second authoritative source where practical |
| Geometry | Overall dimensions, component proportions, orientation, and uncertainty |
| Construction | Materials, finishes, joints, supports, and functional relationships |
| Scientific content | Dataset version, epoch, formula source, molecular entry, or mechanism |
| Rights | Rights status for every reference image, model, scan, texture, and quotation |
| Display conversion | Real dimensions, Roblox dimensions, uniform scale, and disclosed deviations |
| Gameplay fit | Bounding box, collision-shell fit, plaque approach, spawn clearance, and lane clearance |
| Performance target | Mesh, texture, instance, draw, memory, and LOD allocation |
| Review | Researcher, reviewer, status, open uncertainties, and approval date |

Use at least two authoritative sources where practical. Museum catalogue measurements outrank visual estimation. Primary datasets and institutional technical documentation outrank unsourced diagrams, marketplace descriptions, videos, blogs, and generated summaries.

## Source and asset policy

Sources may establish facts without granting permission to import their media. Record facts and media rights separately.

Priority order:

1. original geometry made from verified facts and measurements;
2. institutional CC0 or public-domain models and data;
3. other assets with explicit commercial-use and modification permission;
4. commissioned or paid work only after written user approval.

The Science Museum Group catalogue data can establish titles, makers, dates, materials, and measurements under the rights stated on each record. Do not assume its object photography is licensed for commercial game use.

Smithsonian media is reusable only when the specific item carries the CC0 designation. NASA resources require item-level review of credits, third-party content, insignia, people, and the current NASA media guidelines.

Community models, Creator Store assets, plugins, extensions, and paid marketplace items require provenance, topology, dependency, hidden-content, and security review. A listing title or uploader claim is not proof of ownership.

### Cost approval

Do not purchase, subscribe, commission, or install a paid dependency without explicit user approval. Before approval, report:

- exact item and seller;
- price, currency, taxes or fees if known, and whether the charge recurs;
- license and commercial/derivative-use rights;
- purpose and expected benefit;
- material risks; and
- the best free alternative.

An approved category is not approval for a later purchase. Each cost requires its own approval.

## Production pipeline

1. Complete and approve all 24 research dossiers.
2. Resolve Q-014 and establish a representative lower-end mobile performance baseline.
3. Create the Git LFS-managed `art/` source tree, naming convention, export presets, and backup policy.
4. Build three pilots: the Della Volpaia armillary sphere, Boulton and Watt Lap engine, and NASA-grounded black-hole visualization.
5. Validate factual accuracy, Blender-to-Studio reimport, material behavior, plaque readability, collision-shell fit, and measured performance.
6. Set repeatable per-exhibit geometry and texture budgets from pilot evidence.
7. Produce one sector at a time with separate source commits and Studio version checkpoints.
8. Keep prototype visuals until the corresponding production exhibit passes review; replace them only through a recoverable checkpoint.
9. Run full arena, device, multiplayer, content, rights, and performance acceptance.

Supported exchange formats are FBX, glTF, or OBJ through Roblox's 3D Importer. Preserve unique source object paths so Studio reimport updates the intended MeshParts.
